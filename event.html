Meet Dr. Bryan K. Williams

He started as a busboy at The Ritz-Carlton, St. Thomas. Seventeen roles later, he was the company’s Global Corporate Director of Training & Organizational Effectiveness.

Then he left to build his own firm. Twenty years on, Dr. Bryan has trained and consulted with hundreds of organizations across more than 20 industries — healthcare, luxury hotels, aviation, education, financial services, and government — in 21 countries on six continents.

He is the best-selling author of six books, the founder of the Manager Skills Workshop and the BW Leadership Academy, and served on the Board of Examiners for the Malcolm Baldrige National Quality Award — the highest recognition a U.S. organization can receive for performance excellence.

His passion is simple: help leaders better serve others, so they can better serve the world.

Questions Leaders Ask

How long is each session?
Between 30 and 40 minutes each morning, starting at 10:00 AM EDT on September 17, 18, and 19. Short enough to fit before your workday — built to be worked through, not half-watched.
What time is that where I am?
10:00 AM EDT translates to:
  • 7:00 AM — Los Angeles
  • 3:00 PM — London
  • 4:00 PM — Paris, Madrid, Berlin
  • 6:00 PM — Dubai
  • 10:00 PM — Singapore, Bali, Manila
  • 12:00 AM next day — Sydney
Will there be a replay if I miss a day?
This is a live event, not a recorded course. Dr. Bryan is in the room with you, answering questions in real time — and the daily tools go to those who attend live. Please only claim a seat if you intend to be there.
Do I need to be a senior leader to attend?
No. The room is built for anyone responsible for other people — whether you were promoted last month or have led for twenty years. Dr. Bryan teaches the fundamentals at a level that serves new managers and gives experienced leaders a genuine reset.
Is this going to be a sales pitch?
Three full mornings of teaching, and on the final day Dr. Bryan will share how you can keep working with him if you’d like to. The three days stand entirely on their own whether or not you take him up on it.
What platform is it on, and what do I need?
Zoom, from any laptop, tablet, or phone. Your join link arrives by email the moment you register, and again the morning of each session. Nothing to install beyond Zoom itself.
It’s free — what’s the catch?
There isn’t one. This is a 20th anniversary celebration, and Dr. Bryan wanted to mark it by giving the work away rather than selling it. The only cost is the one that matters: showing up for three mornings.
Can I bring my team?
Yes, and leaders who attend with their team get far more out of it — you leave with shared language instead of solo notes. Each person needs their own seat, and with only 100 available, register together early.
