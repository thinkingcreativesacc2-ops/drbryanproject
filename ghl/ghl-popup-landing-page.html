The Journey

Six Weeks. Six Shifts in How You Lead.

Each week builds on the last — a clear, guided path from where you lead today to the leader you're capable of becoming.

1

Know Your Why

Every 5-star leader leads from conviction, not convenience. This week, you'll get clear on the purpose behind your leadership.

Challenge: Define the "why" that will carry you through hard days.

Handout: Your Leadership Why Worksheet

2

See Your People

People don't follow titles — they follow leaders who truly see them. Learn the practice that builds instant trust.

Challenge: Have one intentional, fully-present conversation.

Handout: The See-Your-People Guide

3

Raise Your Standard

Excellence is a decision, made daily. Discover how to raise the bar for yourself and your team — without burning either out.

Challenge: Identify and correct one "good enough" habit.

Handout: The 5-Star Standard Checklist

4

Develop Someone

Legacy leaders build other leaders. This week is about investing — deliberately — in someone else's growth.

Challenge: Mentor one person through one real challenge.

Handout: The Develop-Someone Action Plan

5

Lead Through the Hard Stuff

Anyone can lead when things are easy. This week equips you to lead with courage and grace when they aren't.

Challenge: Have the conversation you've been avoiding.

Handout: The Difficult Conversations Framework

6

Build What Lasts

A true leader leaves something behind. Close the challenge by defining the legacy you want your leadership to leave.

Challenge: Commit to one lasting leadership practice.

Handout: Your Leadership Legacy Plan
