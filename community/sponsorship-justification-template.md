## Quick Answers if They Push Back

**"We already did leadership training this year."**
That was one day. This is twelve months of follow-through, which is where
behavior actually changes. It's designed to extend one-time training, not
replace it.

**"Will this pull you away from your shifts?"**
No. Both live calls are recorded, the workshop is self-paced, and the weekly
tip video is only a few minutes. It's built for people with real schedules.

**"What do we get for it?"**
A manager who handles conflict earlier, delegates more cleanly, and runs
better one-on-ones — plus whatever I bring back and teach the team each month.

**"Can more of us join?"**
Yes. There's a group registration option if you'd like to enroll several
leaders together.
