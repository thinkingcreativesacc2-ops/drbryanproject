The Philosophy

Rigid things break under pressure. Flexible things endure. It's true in the body. It's true in life.

"Bend Don't Break" isn't just a fitness philosophy — it's a way of moving through the world. It's the belief Greta has built her entire career on, and the foundation of everything inside this community.

Physically

Restoring the flexibility, mobility, and strength that keep you independent for decades to come.

Emotionally

Learning to soften instead of harden — to adapt instead of resist what this season of life is asking of you.

Relationally

Staying open to connection and support — instead of white-knuckling your way through it alone.

For Life

When you learn to bend, you don't just move better. You live better, longer, and more fully.

Meet the Founder

The Woman Behind the Movement

If you came here looking for the answer to the question "Who is Greta B?" I wish you luck. I'm still figuring that out myself!

I sing. I act. I'm a fitness OG. I morph back and forth between my left brain science/longevity world — where I present and moderate panels at prestige conferences — and my right brain can't-stop-performing-creating-hamming it up mode.

Along the way, that same restlessness took me from the founding Editor's desk at Ms. Fitness Magazine to creating FITCAMPS, from Hollywood sets and national morning shows to a nickname or two — The Telomere Diva, and now The Empress of Longevity. Always with the same message underneath it all: your best years are not behind you.

But somewhere along the way, I noticed something. Women didn't just need another workout. They needed a place to belong — women encouraging women, showing up for each other, choosing together to bend instead of break. That place became Band of Bendees.

If you're on this page, I know you can relate. We're unstoppable, you and I.

Questions, Answered

You Might Be Wondering...

Not at all. Band of Bendees was built for women exactly where they are — whether that's returning to movement after years away, or simply wanting more mobility, energy, and community. You don't have to be "fit" to belong here. You just have to be willing to begin.

Then you're in exactly the right place. Most of the women who join Band of Bendees aren't starting from peak fitness — they're starting from wanting more for themselves. Greta built this community for beginnings, not just for women who've already arrived.

This isn't about punishing workouts or unrealistic promises. Band of Bendees is built on decades of real experience in longevity and functional aging, and — more than anything — on genuine community. You're not doing this alone in front of a screen. You're doing it alongside women who understand exactly what this season of life feels like.

It's more than that. While movement and mobility are part of the journey, Band of Bendees is a full community experience — encouragement, longevity education, sustainable habits, and sisterhood, all in one place.

Always. There's no rigid schedule to keep up with and no pressure to perform. Bend Don't Break means honoring your body, your season, and your pace — always.
